Why Whitebox

Don’t just use it.
Understand it.

A black box

  • You can use it.
  • You can’t see how it works.
  • When it breaks, you’re stuck.

A white box

  • You can see every part.
  • You know why it works.
  • You can fix it, change it and build your own.
